## Brand & Style

The design system projects institutional credibility paired with high-velocity product execution. Engineered specifically for Indonesian SMEs, startups, and finance operators, the aesthetic synthesizes banking-grade reliability with contemporary SaaS elegance.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Institutional Authority:** Visual solidity, uncompromising mathematical alignment, and quiet confidence that reassures founders and CFOs managing critical operational capital.
- **Predictive Intelligence:** Subtle indigo accents signal proactive AI automation—such as automated invoice parsing and automated WhatsApp transaction ingestion—without feeling experimental or ungrounded.
- **Clarity Under Density:** Financial decision-makers handle hundreds of ledger rows, runway scenarios, and multi-bank reconciliation feeds daily. The interface prioritizes high signal-to-noise ratio, zero layout shifts, and absolute scanning efficiency.

### Design Movement: Modern Precision SaaS
A disciplined intersection of contemporary minimalism and utilitarian data density. Depth is constructed through crisp micro-borders, stratified slate neutral surfaces, and disciplined atmospheric elevation, rather than heavy skeuomorphism or decorative distraction.

## Colors

The color architecture balances strict financial semantic standards with high-contrast executive reporting.

### Functional Roles & Hierarchies
- **Primary Core (`#059669` Emerald Teal):** Signifies solvency, capital preservation, positive balance deltas, and primary transactional actions. Paired with Slate-950 (`#0B0F19`) for high-contrast typography and structural anchors.
- **Secondary Core (`#4F46E5` Rich Indigo):** Reserved for algorithmic insights, cash burn forecasts, AI tax estimations, and automated reconciliation recommendations.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#25D366` WhatsApp Green):** Exclusively allocated to conversational banking touchpoints, receipt sync notifications, and WhatsApp ledger communication states.
- **Surface Neutrals:** Background canvas starts at `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) and `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100), stepping up to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) on card surfaces to maintain absolute foreground-background separation.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle perimeter lines utilizing `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) and `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) establish crisp grid discipline.

### Semantic State System
- **Inflow / Positive Balance:** Emerald-600 (`#16A34A`) on Soft Mint (`#DCFCE7`).
- **Outflow / Critical Deficit:** Rose-600 (`#E11D48`) on Soft Rose (`#FFE4E6`).
- **Runway Warning / Overdraft Risk:** Amber-600 (`#D97706`) on Soft Amber (`#FEF3C7`).
- **Neutral Holding / Escrow:** Slate-600 (`#475569`) on Slate-100 (`#F1F5F9`).

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans provides geometric modernity alongside open counter-forms, guaranteeing extreme legibility at dense administrative scales.

### Tabular Numerical Architecture
All financial figures, currency prefixes (`Rp`), balance deltas, and percentage metrics must mandate OpenType tabular positioning (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`). This guarantees perfect columnar alignment across ledger rows, eliminating horizontal twitching when balances update in real-time.

### Hierarchy & Letter Spacing
- **Display and Large Balances:** Tightly tracked (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver solid executive summaries.
- **Accounting Headers & Micro Badges:** Driven by `label-caps` in uppercase styling with positive tracking (`+0.06em`) to ensure instant recognition across dense dashboard panes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an 8pt spatial baseline with a dense 4pt sub-grid specifically calibrated for complex data inputs, filter toolbars, and ledger views.

### Screen Adaptability & Grid Structures
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `24px` (`gutter-lg`) spacing and `32px` (`margin-lg`) canvas margin. Accommodates persistent left-rail navigation (240px fixed width), a central high-density transaction workspace, and an optional 360px collapsible intelligence inspector.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `16px` (`gutter`) and `24px` (`margin-md`). Navigation condenses into an icon-rail or off-canvas drawer.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with `16px` margins. Metric summary cards transform into horizontally swipeable carousels; high-density ledger tables collapse into contextual transactional cards showing bank badge, counterparty, and net mutation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on sharp structural surfaces, ghost borders, and calibrated ambient tints rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiering
- **Level 0 (App Shell Canvas):** Solid `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, zero shadows.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Grids):** Solid `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Dropdowns, Filter Drawers, Hover States):** Surface `#FFFFFF` enclosed by 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs, OCR Inspection Lightbox):** Surface `#FFFFFF` with 1px solid `#94A3B8`. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Backdrops use `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.45)` with a `4px` blur filter.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined geometric curve strategy.

- **Base Radius (8px / `0.5rem`):** Standard for form inputs, table containers, action cards, and standard action buttons.
- **Large Radius (12px / `0.75rem`):** Metric KPI modules, modal surfaces, and multi-bank aggregators.
- **Full Radius (`9999px` / Pill):** Applied strictly to dynamic status chips (e.g., Runway Health, WhatsApp Sync Pill, Payment Method Tags) to contrast sharply with architectural tabular containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Financial Action:** Deep Navy (`#0B0F19`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, 8px radius, height `40px` (`36px` in compact tables). Hover transitions to `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary / Action:** Solid `#FFFFFF`, 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive Action:** Soft Rose background (`#FFE4E6`), text `#E11D48`. Hover background `#FECDD3`.

### Metric Summary Cards
- White `#FFFFFF` container with 12px corner radius and 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Top row aligns bank brand avatar (BCA, Mandiri, GoPay, Cash Drawer) alongside an account status dot.
- Numerical aggregate rendered via `title-balance` with Indonesian Rupiah currency mark in muted Slate-500 (`#64748B`).
- Footer displays transactional variance with positive (`#16A34A`) or negative (`#E11D48`) directional indicators and comparison periods.

### WhatsApp Real-time Sync Pill
- Pill-shaped container (`rounded-full`) with `#F0FDF4` background and `#BBF7D0` border.
- Features a ping animation indicator (4px live pulse in `#25D366`) accompanied by `badge-label` text: "WhatsApp Sync: Active (2-way)".

### Budget Guardrail Progress Bar
- Track container: 6px height, rounded-full, `#F1F5F9` background.
- Dynamic fill indicator with explicit threshold states:
  - **Safe (<70%):** `#059669` fill.
  - **Warning (70% - 90%):** `#D97706` fill.
  - **Overbudget (>90%):** `#E11D48` fill with subtle pulse effect.

### Mutasi Transaksi (Ledger Data Table)
- **Header:** Sticky top, background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-caps` in Slate-500 (`#64748B`), bottom border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- **Rows:** Alternating hover highlight to `#F8FAFC`, cell padding `12px 16px`, bottom border 1px solid `#F1F5F9`.
- **Amount Column:** Right-aligned, strict tabular lining numerals (`numeric-table`). Positive cash flow colored `#16A34A` prefixed with `+ Rp`, negative colored `#0F172A` prefixed with `- Rp`.
- **Source Badges:** 
  - `WhatsApp Bot`: Green tint pill with messaging icon.
  - `OCR Nota`: Indigo tint pill with scan aperture icon.
  - `Web Entry`: Slate tint pill with terminal/manual icon.

### Inputs & Filter Bars
- Inputs carry 40px height, 8px radius, `#FFFFFF` background, and 1px `#CBD5E1` outline.
- Active focus invokes a 2px outer ring in Emerald-Teal (`#059669`) with zero layout displacement. Prefix slots support currency denotation (`Rp`) fixed in bold Slate-500.
